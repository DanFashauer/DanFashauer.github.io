About me

Engineering informed by operations.

I'm Daniel (Dan) Fashauer, a Senior Platform Engineer whose career progressed from frontline and systems support into enterprise engineering. That progression shaped my approach: understand the user and operational requirement, identify technical dependencies, validate changes, and make the outcome sustainable for the teams who support it.

My strongest applied background is in Microsoft Intune, Workspace ONE UEM, Jamf, enterprise mobility, Microsoft Entra ID / Active Directory, and Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android platforms. I collaborate with engineering, security, networking, architecture, business, support, and vendor teams to resolve cross-platform problems and support technology transitions.

The endpoint figures above describe the scale of the enterprise environments—not a claim of sole administration or ownership of every device.
