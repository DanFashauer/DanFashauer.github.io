## Evidence model

The portfolio separates evidence into three categories:

- **Core applied experience** - technology and responsibilities used substantially in enterprise roles.
- **Adjacent working knowledge** - technologies or domains Daniel has worked with or understands but does not claim as a deep specialization.
- **Independent concept / developing work** - original architecture or product thinking used to demonstrate reasoning, clearly distinguished from production employer deployments.

Each work sample explicitly identifies whether it is an anonymized reconstruction or independent concept and describes ownership/collaboration boundaries.
