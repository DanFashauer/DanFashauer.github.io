Problem framing

A UEM selection or migration is not only a configuration exercise. Device ownership, enrollment, identity, applications, compliance, network dependencies, support processes, change control, and lifecycle all affect the outcome.

My applied experience

Enterprise endpoint and mobility platforms; Intune, Workspace ONE, Jamf, SCCM/MECM, MobileIron, Autopilot; Windows, macOS, iOS and Android; configuration, testing, troubleshooting, validation, lifecycle and operational standards.

How I contribute

Gather requirements, map dependencies, isolate technical constraints, evaluate implementation trade-offs, test the intended workflow, coordinate specialist/vendor input, document standards, and translate findings into practical recommendations.

What I would measure

Enrollment success, configuration/application reliability, compliance health, recurring incident patterns, deployment quality, support effort, lifecycle completion, and user-impact signals—not merely whether a policy exists.

Ownership & collaboration boundaryI bring substantial endpoint/mobility platform depth and enterprise systems reasoning. Identity, networking, security, application, cloud, and software-development specialists retain deep ownership of their respective domains; my role is to understand the dependencies well enough to make sound platform decisions and coordinate implementation.
